\section{Tensor Products}
Let \(R\) be a ring (not necessarily commutative), \(M, N\) be right and left \(R\)-modules respectively and \(X\) be an abelian group.
\begin{definition}[R-balanced Map]
    A map \(f: M \times N \longrightarrow X\) is called \(R-balanced\) if 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item f(m, n + n') = f(m, n) + f(m, n'),
        \item f(m + m', n) = f(m, n) + f(m', n),
        \item f(rm, n) = f(m, rn),
    \end{itemize}
    for all \(m, m' \in M, n, n' \in N, r \in R\).
\end{definition}
We want to construct most general such \(R\)-balanced map, in the following sense:
\begin{definition}[Tensor Product]
    The \emph{tensor product} of \(M\) and \(N\) over \(R\) is abelian group \(M \otimes_R N\) with an \(R\)-balanced map \[\otimes: M \times N \longrightarrow M \otimes_R N,\] such that following universal property holds true:
    For every \(R\)-balanced map \(f: M \times N \longrightarrow X\), there exists  a unique homomorphism \(\tilde{f}: M \otimes_R N \longrightarrow X\) such that \(f = \tilde{f} \circ \otimes\).
    \[\begin{tikzcd}
	{M \times N} && {M \otimes_R N} \\
	\\
	&& X
	\arrow["\otimes", from=1-1, to=1-3]
	\arrow["f"', from=1-1, to=3-3]
	\arrow["{\exist!\tilde{f}}", dashed, from=1-3, to=3-3]
\end{tikzcd}\]
\end{definition}
\begin{theorem}
    A tensor product of \(M\) and \(N\) exists.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    Let \(\mathbb{Z}(M \times N)\) denoted free abelian group of \(M \times N\).Let \(U \subseteq \mathbb{Z}(M \times N)\) be the subgroup generated by elements \[(m, n + n') - (m, n) - (m, n'), (m + m', n) - (m, n) - (m', n), (rm, n) - (m, rn), \quad \forall m, m' \in M, n, n' \in N, r \in R.\]

    Set \(M \otimes_R N := \mathbb{Z}(M \times N)/U\) and \(m \otimes n := [(m,n)] \in M \otimes_R N\).
\end{proof}
