# Day 2 of solving PhD qualifying exam problem(s): IU Bloomington Tier 1 Algebra Exam (August 2025) Q7

**Question.** Consider a polynomial $f = 2x^{13} - 14x^8 + 42x^6 + 70x^5 - 28x^2 + 490x - 56$.

(a) Let $I_1 = (f)$ be the ideal of $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ generated by $f$. Determine whether the quotient ring  $\mathbb{Q}[x]/I_1$ is a field or not.

(b) Let $I_2 = (f)$ be the ideal of $\mathbb{Z}[x]$ generated by $f$. Determine whether the quotient ring  $\mathbb{Z}[x]/I_2$ is a field or not.
 
**Answer.** 

(a) $\mathbb{Q}[x]/I_1$ is a field iff $I_1 = (f)$ is a maximal ideal iff $f$ is irreducible. So it suffices to prove that $f$ is irreducible. In the polynomial

$$
2x^{13} - 14x^8 + 42x^6 + 70x^5 - 28x^2 + 490x - 56,
$$

since $7$ divides all the coefficients except the leading term (i.e., $2$), and $7^2$ does not divide the constant term (i.e., $56$), using [Eisenstein's Irreducibility Criterion](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/EisensteinsIrreducibilityCriterion.html), $f$ is irreducible.

(b) $\mathbb{Z}[x]/I_2$ is not a field iff $I_2 = (f)$ is not a maximal ideal. Since 

$$
(f) \subset (2) \neq \mathbb{Z}[x]
$$

as $2$ divides $f$, $I_2$ is indeed not maximal. Therefore,  $\mathbb{Z}[x]/I_2$ is not a field.
